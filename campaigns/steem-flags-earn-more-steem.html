## ℹ️ How Can You Earn $STEEM with Steem Flags?


**1️⃣ [Join & Play Steem Flags](https://steemflags.do2earn.dev)**

**2️⃣ Create your own gameplay content using text, screenshots, images, or videos.**

**3️⃣ Publish your content on a Steem platform.**

**4️⃣ Use the tags #steemgame and #steemflags to help the Game Curation Team identify your content.**

**5️⃣ Your content may receive an upvote from the Game Curation Team. If approved, your Steem rewards are generally paid out according to Steem's reward payout schedule.**

<hr>

⚠️ **Receiving an upvote from the Game Curation Team is not guaranteed; however, by consistently producing and publishing high-quality gameplay content about the Steem Flags game on one of the Steem platforms, your gameplay content can get substantial upvote from the team.**

✅ **Support Steem Flags development: When publishing your gameplay content, please consider setting at least 10% Beneficiaries for @do2earn, the developer of Steem Flags.**
